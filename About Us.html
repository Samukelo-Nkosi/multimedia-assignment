About Us

Who We Are

We are the University Printing Department. We help students get their study materials quickly and easily.

We provide Study Guides, Tutorial Workbooks, Practical Manuals and Revision Packs for different university courses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable and quality printing services for students. We aim to ensure that every student has access to the materials they need for studying and examination preparation.

What We Offer

  • Study Guide - R120
  • Tutorial Workbook - R80
  • Practical Manual - R100
  • Revision Pack - R60

Why Choose Us?

  • Affordable prices
  • Fast printing
  • Colour and Black and White options
  • 10% discount when ordering 5 or more copies
  • Easy online ordering

How It Works

  1. Go to the Order Material page.
  2. Enter your name and student number.
  3. Choose your material.
  4. Select the quantity.
  5. Select your printing option.
  6. Submit the order and view your total.
